How Much Does Workplace Exposure Monitoring Cost in the UK?

What sets the cost of a COSHH exposure monitoring survey, what the fixed fee includes and how to get a quote.

Why every survey is quoted on its own

The cost of a workplace exposure survey depends on what is being measured, how many people are sampled and how the work is organised on site. Two workshops of a similar size can need quite different surveys, so we do not publish a price list.

Instead, every survey is quoted as a fixed fee after a short scoping call. The fee includes sampling, analysis at a UKAS-accredited laboratory, travel and the report. Additional samples, and work outside the agreed scope, are charged separately as set out in the quote.

This page sets out what the fixed fee includes and the main factors that affect it, so you know what we will ask about when you get in touch.

What Is Included

What the Fixed Fee Includes

Every workplace exposure survey is quoted as a single fixed fee after a short scoping call.

Scoping call

A short call to understand the processes, the substances of concern, the number of workers and the site layout. The sampling strategy is agreed before the quote is issued.

Sampling on site

Personal breathing-zone sampling, with static samples where they are needed, over a representative period of the working shift. Sampling pumps are flow-checked before and after sampling with a calibrated flowmeter.

Laboratory analysis

Analysis of every sample at a UKAS-accredited laboratory, using the method that suits the substance: gravimetric weighing for inhalable and respirable dust, X-ray diffraction (XRD) for respirable crystalline silica, and the relevant methods for metals and solvent vapours.

Travel

Travel to and from the site is included in the fixed fee.

The report

A written report comparing each result with the Workplace Exposure Limits in HSE document EH40, with recommendations on monitoring strategy. The draft report is ready for issue within 10 working days of receipt of the laboratory results.

Cost Drivers

What affects the cost

The main factors that set the fixed fee for a workplace exposure survey.

Number of workers and sampling positions We sample several workers in each similar-exposure group. More job roles, process areas or static sampling positions means more samples and more time on site.
Substances and type of analysis The sampling method and laboratory analysis depend on the substance: gravimetric analysis for inhalable and respirable dust, respirable crystalline silica (RCS) by X-ray diffraction, metals by ICP analysis, and solvent vapours on sorbent tubes. Some substances need more than one sample per worker.
Short-term samples Where a substance has a 15-minute short-term exposure limit, or a task such as cleaning down gives peak exposures, short-term samples are added alongside the longer samples.
Static and personal sampling Personal samples show what each worker breathes. Static samples at fixed positions are added where they help show where the dust or vapour comes from, and each one adds to the analysis.
Shift pattern and night work Where exposure differs between day and night shifts, or work runs over several shifts, more than one visit may be needed to cover a representative period of each.
Site location Site location affects travel time and the length of the site day. Travel is included in the fixed fee.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a basic workplace dust survey cost?

Every survey is quoted as a fixed fee after a short scoping call, because the cost depends on the number of workers and sampling positions, the substances and type of analysis, the shift pattern and the site location. The fixed fee includes sampling, analysis at a UKAS-accredited laboratory, travel and the report. Additional samples, and work outside the agreed scope, are charged separately as set out in the quote.

Is the lab cost included in your quote?

Yes. Analysis at a UKAS-accredited laboratory is included in the fixed fee, together with sampling, travel and the report. You do not pay the laboratory separately.

Do you charge for travel?

Travel is included in the fixed fee. It is not charged separately.

How long does the report take?

The draft report is ready for issue within 10 working days of receipt of the laboratory results.

Can I get a fixed quote without a site visit?

Yes. A short scoping call about the substances, the processes and the number of workers is normally enough to issue a fixed-fee quote.

Tell us the substances, worker numbers and site location — we will send back a written fixed-fee quote within 24 hours. We work right across the UK.
